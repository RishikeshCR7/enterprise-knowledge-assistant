Enterprise Sales Compensation & Commission Policy 2026

Department: Sales
Security Clearance: Confidential
Owner: Sales Operations

Policy Overview:
Account Executives earn a base commission rate of 10% on closed-won Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) up to 100% quota attainment. Accelerators kick in at 15% for quota attainment between 100%-150%, and 20% for quota attainment above 150%. Quarterly sales performance bonuses of $10,000 disburse upon achieving regional team revenue targets. Commission payouts occur monthly on the 15th following deal invoice reconciliation.
